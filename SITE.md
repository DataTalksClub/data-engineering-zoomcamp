Data Engineering Zoomcamp is a free, intensive course that takes you from data engineering fundamentals to building production-grade data pipelines with industry-standard tools. No previous data engineering experience is required.

The course runs for ten weeks: seven weeks of modules covering containerization and infrastructure as code, workflow orchestration, data warehousing, analytics engineering, batch processing, and streaming, followed by three weeks for a final project. Each module has video lectures, reading material, and a homework assignment.
